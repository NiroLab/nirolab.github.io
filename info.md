## About (from about.md)
Innovation hub where creativity meets cutting-edge research. Mission: make robotics research accessible, impactful, future-focused. Structured **Robotics Innovation Cycle** with six stages: Modelling & Simulation → Design → Fabrication → Assembling → Software Development → Physical Testing (iterative loop). Vision: cornerstone of a self-sustaining robotics ecosystem in Bangladesh; bridge academia and industry.

**Research areas (8):** AI-driven robotics; Multi-Robot Systems; Edge AI computing for robotic systems; Autonomous & context-aware robotics; Aerial and Underwater Robotics; Adaptive decision-making in robotics; Uncertainty quantification and inference in robotics; Integration of IoT, Edge & cloud computing, and blockchain into robotics.

**Mission pillars:** Research Excellence; Education & Training; Collaboration; Innovation; Community Building.
