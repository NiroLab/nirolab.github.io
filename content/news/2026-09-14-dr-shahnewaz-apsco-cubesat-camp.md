We are delighted to share that Dr. Shahnewaz Siddique, Director of NIRO Lab, and our lab's research team have been featured by [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/education/419527/dr-shahnewaz-leads-bangladesh-team-at-apsco) for their participation in the APSCO CubeSat Competition (ACC) Summer Camp in Xi'an, China. The camp was held from August 12 to 25, 2026, and marked another important milestone in our lab's ongoing CubeSat and space-technology research.

![Bangladesh Team at the APSCO CubeSat Competition Summer Camp](/pictures/news/dr-shahnewaz-apsco.png)

The featured work was carried out by our research team under the leadership of Dr. Shahnewaz. Organized under the Asia-Pacific Space Cooperation Organization (APSCO), the summer camp provided participants with hands-on training in key CubeSat subsystems. The program covered onboard computing, control systems, communications, power management, structural integration, thermal management, and ground-station interfacing. The training was hosted by Northwestern Polytechnical University, one of China's leading institutions for aerospace and engineering education.

Our research team earned its place in the program through a rigorous selection process coordinated by the Bangladesh Space Research and Remote Sensing Organization (SPARRSO), the country's focal agency for APSCO. Following SPARRSO's call for participation, the team submitted detailed CubeSat design proposals, which were evaluated and approved by an expert panel from APSCO.

With the summer camp successfully completed, Dr. Shahnewaz and the team will now advance to the next phase of the competition: building, testing, and validating the engineering model of their proposed CubeSat. This important stage will transform the approved design into a functional prototype and demonstrate its readiness for further development.

This achievement reflects NIRO Lab's continued commitment to hands-on research, technical innovation, and the advancement of space-technology education in Bangladesh. We congratulate Dr. Shahnewaz and every member of the research team, and we look forward to sharing further updates as they progress through the next phase of the competition.
