The NSU Intelligent Robotics Lab (NIRO Lab) is proud to announce that our flagship assistive robotics project, **NIRO BIT**, has been officially recognized among the **TOP 50 Innovations** at the prestigious **[Bangladesh Innovation Fair 2026](https://innovationfairbd.org/)**.

Organized by the **Ministry of Science & Technology**, Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh, the three-day national event took place from **September 12 to 14, 2026**, at the **Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Novo Theatre (Novotheatre), Dhaka**. Centered around the theme *"Innovate to Market"*, the fair brought together researchers, tech pioneers, and industry leaders across 14 high-impact sectors to bridge the gap between academic innovation and commercial readiness.

![NIRO Lab receiving the Certificate of Recognition at the Bangladesh Innovation Fair 2026 Closing Ceremony](/pictures/news/niro-bit-bangladesh-innovation-fair-award.jpg)

## Recognizing NIRO BIT in AI, Automation & Robotics

Selected under the **Artificial Intelligence, Automation & Robotics** category, **NIRO BIT** (*An AI-Powered Spatial-Intelligence Vision-Robotic Home Assistant System*) represents NIRO Lab's commitment to creating accessible, socially meaningful robotics solutions. 

The project has been developed by research team members **Md. Yasin Arafat** and **Farhana Mehazbin Tusti** under the guidance of NIRO Lab Director **Dr. Shahnewaz Siddique**.

NIRO BIT is engineered specifically to support elderly individuals and people with physical disabilities in performing day-to-day tasks safely, independently, and with dignity. The system integrates:

- **Modular ROS2 Architecture**: Built on NVIDIA Jetson embedded computing and custom STM32 motor control protocols.
- **Multimodal Perception & Computer Vision**: Utilizing YOLOv5, NanoTrack, and MediaPipe for object detection, human tracking, and gesture recognition.
- **Indoor Autonomous Navigation**: Powered by SLAM Toolbox and Nav2 for real-time mapping, waypoint localization, and dynamic obstacle avoidance using omnidirectional mecanum wheels.
- **Robotic Manipulation**: A 6-DOF robotic arm utilizing inverse kinematics and PID closed-loop control for pick-and-place assistance.
- **Cognitive & Voice Interaction**: Large Language Model (LLM/VLLM) contextual reasoning paired with natural voice command processing and automated fall-detection capabilities.

## Three Days of Live Demonstrations & Industry Engagement

Throughout the exhibition, the NIRO Lab team hosted an interactive booth featuring live physical demonstrations of the NIRO BIT prototype alongside real-time software and navigation feeds. 

The stall witnessed enthusiastic engagement from visitors, university researchers, tech entrepreneurs, corporate leaders, and visiting government delegations. Attendees witnessed firsthand how NIRO BIT interprets human commands, tracks target objects, plans collision-free trajectories in crowded indoor setups, and carries out assistive tasks.

![Live demonstrations, visitor interactions, and dignitary walkthroughs at the NIRO BIT stall](/pictures/news/niro-bit-bangladesh-innovation-fair-communications.jpeg)

## Formal Recognition at the Closing Ceremony

At the grand Closing Ceremony held on **September 14, 2026**, the NIRO Lab team was formally presented with the **Certificate of Recognition** celebrating NIRO BIT's selection into the nationwide **TOP 50 Innovations**. 

This honor highlights not only the technical ingenuity and engineering rigor behind the project, but also its tangible market potential in addressing elderly and disability care challenges in Bangladesh and internationally.

## The Path Forward: Innovate to Market

Aligned with the fair's ethos of taking homegrown research from the lab bench to real-world deployment, the NIRO BIT team is advancing toward the next developmental milestones:
- Deploying quantized, on-device multimodal models for low-latency offline edge reasoning.
- Expanding comprehensive Bengali voice command interaction (Bangla ASR).
- Conducting in-home trial evaluations with healthcare and rehabilitation stakeholders.

We extend our heartfelt congratulations to **Md. Yasin Arafat**, **Farhana Mehazbin Tusti**, and **Dr. Shahnewaz Siddique** for this outstanding achievement, and we thank the Ministry of Science & Technology and the fair organizers for celebrating technological innovation across Bangladesh.
