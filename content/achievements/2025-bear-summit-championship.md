The Autonomous Indoor Delivery Robot built by NIRO Lab earned the Championship Award at BEAR Summit 2025 - the lab's first national title, less than a year after its founding. The award, in the summit's Robotics Demo Digital Display segment, recognized the prototype's autonomous navigation, perception pipeline, and integrated web-based control interface.
