## Overview

Bangladesh is overwhelmingly an agriculture-based country, and the future of farming here lies in smarter, more efficient ways to tend the land. This project reimagines how we monitor crops from above — bringing the power of aerial insight directly to the fields.

Our fixed-wing aerial platform is built specifically for Bangladesh's unique landscapes, climate, and farming traditions. It soars over farmland to capture a bird's-eye view of crop health and growth, giving farmers and agricultural experts an entirely new perspective on their fields. Rather than walking row by row, this eye-in-the-sky approach reveals patterns, problem areas, and opportunities that simply aren't visible from the ground.

By embracing precision agriculture, farmers can grow more with less — reducing water use, minimizing pesticide application, and ultimately harvesting better yields while being kinder to the environment. It's a win for productivity, a win for costs, and a win for the planet.

## Mission

To empower Bangladesh's agricultural community with an accessible, efficient aerial monitoring solution that transforms how we understand and manage our farmland — leading to healthier crops, lower costs, and a more sustainable future for farming.
