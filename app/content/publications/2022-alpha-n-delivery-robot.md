Alpha-N presents an automated delivery robot that computes shortest paths while detecting and avoiding obstacles in real time. The work was completed during Md. Saif Ahammod Khan's time at North South University, combining path-planning algorithms with a low-cost mobile robot platform for last-meter delivery tasks.
